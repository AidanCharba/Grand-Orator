Master Challenge

Record a 60–90 second performance on a concrete topic. Demonstrate at least three techniques, then evaluate the recording against the rubric.

1. Clear purpose

The audience can identify your main point.

2. Vocal technique

Use deliberate emphasis, pauses, and controlled projection.

3. Interpretation

Your delivery fits the meaning rather than sounding exaggerated.
